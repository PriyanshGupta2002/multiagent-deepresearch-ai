# Report Writing Skill

## Purpose

Write professional research reports that feel natural,
clear, analytical, and engaging.

The objective is to communicate research findings
as an expert human researcher would.

## 1. Natural Writing

- Use natural sentence structures.
- Vary sentence length and paragraph structure.
- Avoid repetitive sentence openings.
- Avoid generic AI-style introductions.
- Avoid unnecessary filler and exaggerated language.
- Prefer precise and meaningful wording.

## 2. Narrative Flow

- Build a logical progression between sections.
- Introduce concepts before discussing complex details.
- Connect findings with relevant context.
- Ensure each section contributes to the overall argument.
- Use transitions naturally.

## 3. Evidence Integration

- Support factual claims with available research.
- Preserve source URLs and references.
- Integrate statistics with explanations.
- Never fabricate citations or evidence.
- Distinguish facts, interpretations, and uncertainty.

## 4. Analytical Writing

- Explain why important findings matter.
- Compare different perspectives where relevant.
- Discuss implications and trade-offs.
- Highlight conflicting evidence.
- Avoid presenting unsupported conclusions.

## 5. Readability

- Use descriptive headings.
- Keep paragraphs focused.
- Use bullet points only when they improve clarity.
- Avoid excessive lists.
- Explain technical terminology when necessary.

## 6. Conclusions

- Summarize the most significant findings.
- Connect conclusions to the original research question.
- Provide recommendations only when supported by evidence.
- Acknowledge important limitations.

## 7. Writing Integrity

- Never invent facts, statistics, sources, or quotations.
- Never fabricate personal experiences or anecdotes.
- Never hide conflicting evidence.
- Never sacrifice accuracy for readability.

## Final Quality Standard

The report should read like carefully prepared professional
research, not a collection of AI-generated bullet points.
