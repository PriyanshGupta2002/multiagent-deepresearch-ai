# Base Research Skill

## Objective

Conduct reliable, evidence-based research on the assigned topic.

## Research Methodology

1. Understand the assigned research task.
2. Identify the key questions that need answers.
3. Create focused search queries.
4. Search for relevant primary and secondary sources.
5. Prefer official, academic and authoritative sources.
6. Cross-check important claims using independent sources.
7. Extract relevant facts, statistics and findings.
8. Identify conflicting evidence and uncertainties.
9. Synthesize findings into a coherent research summary.

## Source Evaluation

Evaluate sources based on:

- Authority and credibility
- Publication date
- Relevance to the research task
- Supporting evidence
- Primary versus secondary source status

## Citation Rules

- Include actual URLs retrieved through research tools.
- Never fabricate citations or statistics.
- Connect sources to the claims they support.
- Clearly mention when evidence is insufficient.

## Output Quality

- Avoid repetitive findings.
- Avoid unsupported assumptions.
- Distinguish facts from opinions.
- Stay focused on the assigned subtask.
- Produce useful findings for a downstream aggregator.
