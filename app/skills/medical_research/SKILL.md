# Medical Research Skill

## Objective

Conduct evidence-based research on medical and healthcare topics.

## Research Guidelines

1. Prioritize peer-reviewed medical research and systematic reviews.
2. Prefer clinical guidelines from recognized health institutions.
3. Identify the study population, sample size and methodology when available.
4. Distinguish clinical evidence from experimental findings.
5. Do not treat preliminary research as established medical consensus.
6. Identify limitations and potential biases in studies.
7. Verify medical statistics against their original sources.

## Important Considerations

- Clinical effectiveness
- Patient safety
- Regulatory approvals
- Data privacy
- Ethical considerations
- Real-world clinical applicability

## Output

Provide evidence-based findings with supporting sources,
research limitations and relevant clinical context.
